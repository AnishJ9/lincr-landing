How It Works

LINCR

One platform, four steps — from case posted to patient cleared

01

Surgical Center Posts a Case

Surgery center submits patient details and surgery date. The case lands in the LINCR queue in under a minute.

02

Patient Gets a Text & Picks a Time

Patient receives an SMS with their intake link — no app download. They complete medical history on their phone and tap the windows that work for their pre-op video visit.

03

Provider Claims It

Credentialed anesthesia providers browse available cases, pick a slot the patient already opened, and claim the case. Work from anywhere, on your schedule.

04

Evaluate & Clear

Provider conducts the video visit, reviews the patient intake, and signs the pre-anesthesia evaluation and surgical clearance note.

Pricing · ROI

$250 in. $5,600 out.

No subscription, no implementation fee, no per-seat license. Pay per cleared case — only when a credentialed anesthesia provider signs the note.

What you pay
$250/ cleared case

Flat per-case fee. Zero charge if a case isn't cleared in time. Provider keeps $150, LINCR keeps $100 to operate the marketplace.

  • Signed pre-anesthesia clearance, ready in your portal
  • Patient intake collected via SMS — no app download
  • Outcome tracking + missed-case dashboard included
  • HIPAA-compliant video, BAA on request
What one prevented cancellation saves
$5,850midpoint lost per case

A same-day cancellation in an ASC burns about $5,850 on average — the midpoint of $65/min in OR cost across a 90-minute case. That covers the lost OR slot, idle staff, prepped supplies, and the scramble to rebook.

  • Reclaim 1–2 OR slots per week from preventable cancellations
  • Stop chasing patients for missing meds, EKGs, labs
  • Push pre-op work off your nurse's desk
  • Documented clearance trail for QI / audit
