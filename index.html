Product

LINCR

Pre-anesthesia evaluation with a quality feedback loop

Surgical clearance that improves with every case

01

SMS/Email Patient Intake

Text or email patients a link. Adaptive questionnaire adapts in real-time based on their responses. No app download required.

02

Risk Scoring + Note Generation

Complete pre-anesthesia evaluation and clearance note in 90 seconds. ASA classification, cardiac risk, airway, OSA, medication alerts — all automated.

03

Smart Triage

Automated Green / Yellow / Red classification. Routes patients to the right level of evaluation before clearance.

04

Missed-Case Feedback

After surgery, record outcomes. LINCR identifies cases that were triaged green but cancelled or had complications — your quality feedback loop.
