Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 19, 2026

LINCR ("LINCR," "we," "us," or "our") operates a platform that enables healthcare facilities, providers, and patients to coordinate pre-operative clearance. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information that healthcare facilities, providers, and patients provide to us, including:

  • Facility and provider account information: name, email address, organization name, and role.
  • Patient information submitted by facilities: name, date of birth, phone number, scheduled procedure, and clinical details necessary to complete pre-operative clearance.
  • Patient intake responses: health history and clinical information submitted by patients through intake links.
  • Communications data: SMS delivery status and visit telemetry necessary to operate the service.

How We Use Information

  • To provide the LINCR pre-operative clearance workflow, including case routing, patient intake, telehealth visits, and clearance documentation.
  • To send patients transactional SMS messages containing secure intake links and telehealth visit links related to a clearance request initiated by a healthcare facility on the patient's behalf.
  • To maintain audit logs and security monitoring.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

SMS Messaging

LINCR sends transactional SMS messages to patients whose phone numbers have been submitted by a referring healthcare facility as part of a pre-operative clearance request. Patients opt in when a facility confirms, on the patient's behalf, that the patient has consented to receive SMS related to their clearance.

Message frequency varies based on clearance activity. Standard message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any LINCR message to opt out of future SMS. Reply HELP for support.

We do not share phone numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Phone numbers are used solely to deliver transactional messages required to complete the clearance workflow.

How We Share Information

We share information only as follows:

  • With the referring facility and clearing provider associated with a case, as necessary to complete clearance.
  • With service providers that power the platform (for example, cloud hosting, authentication, SMS delivery, and telehealth video), bound by contractual obligations to handle data only for the purposes we direct.
  • When required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of LINCR, our users, or the public.

We do not sell personal information.

Health Information

LINCR is designed to support pre-operative clearance workflows. Where applicable, LINCR handles protected health information ("PHI") under Business Associate Agreements with covered entities, in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA).

Data Retention

We retain case information, intake responses, and clearance notes for the period required by applicable law and for the operational needs of the referring facility and clearing provider. Audit logs are retained for security and compliance purposes.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information against loss, unauthorized access, and disclosure, including encryption in transit, access controls, and audit logging. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

  • SMS opt-out: Reply STOP to any LINCR SMS message.
  • Access, correction, or deletion: Contact us at the address below. If you are a patient, please contact the referring facility directly when possible, as they control your case record.

Children's Privacy

LINCR is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Pediatric patient information submitted by a referring facility is handled under the facility's authority as the covered entity.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.
