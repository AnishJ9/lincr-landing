Patient

Complete your pre-op intake

You're the patient. Your surgery center texted you a link. No password, no app download — just a short form to share your medical history, medications, and lifestyle so your anesthesiologist can clear you before surgery day.

  • Open the intake link (no login)
  • Answer a few screening questions
  • Submit → provider gets notified
Token-authed Link is fetched live from the demo API
Launch patient intake →

About this environment. The demo runs against a live deployment with one seeded facility, one seeded provider, and one open case. Sign-in credentials are shared by the LINCR team; the patient link is generated dynamically. If something looks off, it's probably because the seed was just re-run — refresh and try again.
